NORTHSTAR NOTES — FICTIONAL SUPPORT HANDBOOK
Version 1.0 | Demo dataset | October 10, 2026

ABOUT THIS DOCUMENT
Northstar Notes is an invented note-taking service. All products, prices,
policies, incidents, and contact details below are fictional. This handbook
is original sample content for a public local AI document-search demo.
It describes no real company's policies and contains no customer records.
Permission is granted to use, copy, modify, and redistribute this handbook,
including in public repositories and demonstrations.

1. SERVICE AND PLANS
Northstar Notes lets users write notes, organize notebooks, and export files.
The Free plan includes one user, three notebooks, and 100 MB of storage.
The Plus plan costs 8 USD per month and includes one user, unlimited notebooks,
and 5 GB of storage. The Team plan costs 12 USD per user per month and includes
shared notebooks and 10 GB of storage per paid user. There is no annual plan.
These fictional prices exclude any applicable taxes.

2. PASSWORD RESETS
To reset a password, select Forgot password on the sign-in page and enter
the account email address. A reset link expires 30 minutes after it is issued.
Requesting a new link invalidates older links. If no message arrives within
five minutes, check spam and verify that the email address was entered correctly.
Support must never request a password or a password-reset link.
Accounts created using an external sign-in provider must reset their password
with that provider, rather than through Northstar Notes.

3. TWO-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION AND LOCKOUTS
Two-factor authentication uses an authenticator app. If a code is rejected,
enable automatic time synchronization on the device and try a fresh code.
A saved recovery code can be used once. Never share recovery codes with support.
After five failed password attempts within ten minutes, password sign-in is
locked for 15 minutes. Further attempts do not shorten the lockout.
If both the authenticator and recovery codes are unavailable, contact support
for identity verification. Recovery is not guaranteed, and verification cannot
be bypassed by providing payment-card details.

4. BILLING AND DUPLICATE CHARGES
Paid plans renew monthly on the subscription date. If that date does not exist
in a month, renewal occurs on the month's last day.
A pending card authorization is not necessarily a second completed charge.
For two completed charges covering the same account and billing period,
support checks the transaction records and refunds a confirmed duplicate.
Provide the invoice number, charge date, amount, and only the last four digits
of the card. Never send a full card number, security code, or banking password.
Receipts are available under Settings > Billing > Invoices.

5. REFUND POLICY
The first payment for a new paid subscription is eligible for a refund if the
request is submitted within 14 calendar days of that payment.
Routine renewal payments are not refundable under this fictional policy.
Confirmed duplicate charges are refundable even outside the 14-day window.
Once approved, refunds are submitted to the original payment method within
two business days. The bank may take another five to ten business days to
display the credit. These are separate stages, not an immediate refund.
Support cannot promise an exact bank posting date.

6. CANCELLATION AND DOWNGRADES
Cancel under Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription.
Cancellation stops the next renewal; paid access continues until the current
billing period ends. Cancellation does not automatically create a refund.
At the end of the paid period, the account moves to the Free plan.
If an account exceeds Free-plan limits, existing notes remain readable and
exportable, but creating notebooks or uploading files is blocked until the
account is within the limits or a paid plan is restored.

7. SYNCHRONIZATION PROBLEMS
When a recent note does not appear on another device, first confirm that both
devices use the same account. Check the internet connection and refresh the app.
Inspect the synchronization indicator before assuming that changes were saved.
If the problem continues, record the app version, device type, approximate time,
and exact error message. Copy any unsynchronized text to a local file before
signing out, clearing browser data, or reinstalling the app.
Do not delete the local copy until the note appears correctly on both devices.
An offline edit does not synchronize until the device reconnects.

8. FILE UPLOADS AND STORAGE
Supported attachments are PDF, TXT, PNG, and JPG files.
The maximum size of a single attachment is 20 MB on every plan.
If an upload fails, check its size and file type, then check available storage.
Renaming an unsupported file extension does not convert the file.
When storage is full, reading and exporting existing notes still work.
New attachments are blocked until space is freed or the plan is upgraded.
Password-protected PDFs can be stored, but their contents are not indexed.

9. SEARCH AND DOCUMENT EXTRACTION
Search indexes note text and text extracted from unencrypted PDFs and TXT files.
Image-only scanned PDFs require optical character recognition before their
contents can be searched. This fictional service does not provide built-in OCR.
A PDF page number refers to its physical page position starting at one; it
may differ from the page number printed inside the document.
Newly uploaded searchable files normally appear in search within two minutes.
Search results identify matching passages; they do not prove every statement
in a document is true. Users should inspect the cited source before acting.

10. EXPORTS AND DELETED NOTES
Export a notebook under Notebook menu > Export. Available formats are TXT
and Markdown. Attachments are included as separate files in the export archive.
Deleted notes remain in Trash for 30 calendar days unless permanently deleted
earlier by the user. Restore them under Trash > Select note > Restore.
After permanent deletion or expiration of the 30-day period, normal account
tools cannot restore the note. Support must not promise recovery.
Export important material before closing an account.

11. TEAM PERMISSIONS
Owners manage billing, invite members, and transfer ownership.
Editors create and modify shared notes but cannot change billing.
Viewers read shared notes but cannot edit or invite members.
Only an owner can change a member's role. Removing a team member revokes
their shared-notebook access but does not delete their personal notebooks.
Every team must have at least one owner.

12. SUPPORT HOURS AND PRIORITIES
Fictional support hours are Monday through Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 UTC,
excluding fictional company holidays. The example contact address is
support@northstar-notes.example; it is a placeholder, not a working inbox.
Priority 1: widespread inability to sign in or access saved notes.
Priority 2: repeated synchronization failures affecting several users.
Priority 3: an isolated billing question, upload issue, or feature request.
Initial response targets during staffed support hours are one hour for
Priority 1, four hours for Priority 2, and two business days for Priority 3.
Response targets describe an initial reply, not guaranteed resolution times.

13. INCIDENT HANDLING AND PRIVACY
For a suspected outage, check the fictional status page at
status.northstar-notes.example and collect the time and exact error message.
Never instruct a user to disable account security as an outage workaround.
Support tickets should contain only information needed to diagnose the issue.
Redact passwords, reset links, recovery codes, full card numbers, and private
note contents from screenshots and logs before sharing them.
The service does not have an established recovery-time guarantee in this
demo handbook. Do not invent one when answering incident questions.
